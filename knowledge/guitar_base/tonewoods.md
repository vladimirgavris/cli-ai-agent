Alder: balanced tone, lightweight, common on Stratocasters. Ash: bright with strong low end; swamp ash is lighter and resonant. Mahogany: warm, heavy, strong midrange; common on Les Pauls. Basswood: soft, neutral tone, budget-friendly, dents easily. A maple cap is often glued atop mahogany bodies for added brightness and hardness. Finish type is often claimed to affect tone, but many builders consider its effect negligible compared to wood choice and construction.
